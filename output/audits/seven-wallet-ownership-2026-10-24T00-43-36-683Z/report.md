## 4. Patterns worth reviewing

1. **Synchronized first participation:** all seven’s first recorded Rounds votes were on 23 October, between **19:50:44 and 20:24:45 UTC** — about 34 minutes, close to the 21:00 deadline.
2. **Shared priorities:** 105 votes to Support Yellow Artists, 66 to The Culture We Own, and 72 to We can make it!: **243 / 360 votes (67.5%)** went to those three.
3. **Similar final ballots:** miniom and desdot chose exactly the same five entries. Both gave 15 to Support, 15 to We can make it!, and 1 to The Culture We Own; their remaining weights differed.
4. **Two voting batches:** the first three wallets gave Paint the world Yellow no votes; all four later wallets supported it. This is a descriptive pattern, not independent proof of coordination.
5. **Established financial links:** the prior scan found 194 direct ETH transactions plus one internal transfer among the seven. AmirK/Mahnaz exchanged ETH 79 times on Base; this follow-up adds **43 on Ethereum**.

**Counterevidence:** Their Collective Nouns were acquired in 2024–2025, well before this round. No full ballots were identical. Using a full budget or 15-vote cap is common: **35/52 other voters** used their full budgets and **46/52** placed at least one 15-vote allocation. Those behaviors alone are weak signals.

## Recommendation

Prioritize an explanation of the seven wallets’ control and coordinated timing from the AmirK/Mahnaz-associated group. Hellia’s repeat support makes the relationship with the Support Yellow Artists submitter worth clarifying. **The available evidence does not justify naming that submitter as the owner, removing votes as proven self-votes, or disqualifying anyone on this finding alone.**
