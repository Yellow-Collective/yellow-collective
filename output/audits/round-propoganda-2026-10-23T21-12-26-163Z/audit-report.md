# Yellow Propoganda — final vote audit

**Recommendation: review the seven-wallet late cluster and the two submitter-linked groups before awards. Submitter-controlled self-voting is not proven.**

Voting ended **October 23, 2026, 5 p.m. Eastern**. Audit cutoff: **5:12:26 p.m. Eastern**. Checked **59 voters, 308 allocations, 2,950 votes and 22 active entries**. All 59 had sufficient historical voting power. **Zero direct self-votes, entry-cap violations, wallet-budget violations or votes recorded after close.** Stored winners reconcile exactly with the final ballots.

The **new seven-wallet cluster** first participated in Rounds within **34 minutes** on the final day. Each gave Support Yellow Artists 15 votes. Their historical ETH transfers connect all seven, and two exchanged NFT 399. This supports closer investigation of coordination/common operation, but does not establish that any beneficiary submitter controls them. Their activity predates this round by years.

**Paint:** both b897/79ed now supported the same submitter who transferred NFTs 870/860 to them before the previous round. **Slop:** the new Etherscan evidence shows 3ba received ETH from its beneficiary submitter and sent ETH back four times; its 30 votes are additional to 4e2a's existing 34-vote NFT-transfer lead. All are relationships requiring explanation, not confirmed cheating.

## Conditional winner impact

**These are review scenarios; no votes were removed.**

- If all **360 votes from the seven-wallet cluster** were excluded, **Draw or Die would move 4th → 1st**, and **Support Yellow Artists 1st → 5th (318 → 213 votes)**. The same ten entries would remain winners.
- If only that group's **105 votes for Support Yellow Artists** were excluded, it would move **1st → 6th**. Perpetual Collective Machine would become first. This narrower scenario models a beneficiary-specific decision, not a recommendation to select one beneficiary's votes without supporting policy/evidence.
- Removing only the **30 Paint votes** would move Paint **3rd → 4th**.
- Removing the earlier **120 votes** would move Museum **9th → 10th**; We can make it would remain sixth.

## Decision

**Do not treat these counts as proven self-votes.** Ask for the wallet relationships behind F01–F03 before awarding. The audit supports review; it does not establish a disqualification. All detailed ballot IDs, arithmetic, conditional rankings, chain receipts and verification are saved with this report.

Production access was read-only. No votes, winners or awards were changed. No SSH keys or provider settings were changed during this rerun. The earlier audit remains intact.
