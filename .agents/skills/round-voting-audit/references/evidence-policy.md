# Evidence policy

OBSERVED means supported by a saved DB row, verified chain event/receipt, or public profile; cite IDs, blocks/times, hashes or URLs. INFERRED means a plausible explanation such as common control; state alternatives and what evidence could refute it. UNKNOWN means missing, blocked, stale or incomplete evidence.

Wallets are addresses, not people. Voting patterns, profiles, NFT transfers, common funding and delegation are distinct signals; none alone proves common control. Apply rules that were in force for the audited round. Unknown is not zero; incomplete histories cannot prove absence.

Record round ID/slug, production project/service, code revision, UTC cutoff, read-only assertion, chain, contract, block ranges, row grain/counts, joins, orphan/excluded rows, winners/moderation reconciliation, provider pagination/failures and rerun command.

Keep the report decision-first and concise. Link candidate Yellow profiles and BaseScan pages. Give own-entry vote counts and evidence confidence. Group by an owner only when supported; otherwise say Owner unknown. Put full histories/arithmetic in JSON. State no votes were changed unless an authorized separate action occurred. Winner effects are conditional scenarios, never a claim that records changed.

Use historical chain state at the eligibility snapshot or ballot time. Check Transfer and delegation events, verify important receipts, and declare coverage. Receipt of an NFT can be a gift, sale, custody, trade or distribution.
